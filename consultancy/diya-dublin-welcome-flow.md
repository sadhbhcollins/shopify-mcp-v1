## Notes for the build
- **Colours:** pull from her site and brand (bright pinks, oranges, blues on a clean white background)
- **Fonts:** match her site, or use a clean sans-serif
- **Images:** use her product photos from the site for the pitch mock-ups
- **Mobile first:** single column, big buttons, short paragraphs
- **Expiry:** "expires in 48 hours" only works if the code is set to expire; otherwise change to "while it lasts"
