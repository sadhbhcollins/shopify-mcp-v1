# Diya Dublin: first-look audit notes

*Reviewed 17 Nov 2026 from https://www.diyadublin.com (homepage + Markets page).*

## What she sells
Colourful handmade-style products with playful, food-themed names (Raspberry Cheesecake, Strawberry Matcha, Orange Soda Tiger, Pink Grapefruit):
- **Quilted laptop totes** (~€38), **shoulder bags** (~€36), crossbody bags (~€28)
- **Reversible quilted jackets** (~€74), sherpa (~€68)
- Washbags, make-up pouches, coin and pencil pouches
- **Home:** cushion covers (~€12), table runners (~€32), table covers (~€62), placemats
- **Blue Pottery** plates and bowls (€5–€25), **Kashmir enamels**, earrings, gift cards
- Also sells at Dublin markets (Temple Bar Square, Fade Street) and wholesale

**Tone:** fun, bright, foodie-sweet names. The emails should feel colourful and playful, not corporate.

## Strengths (say these first in the pitch)
- **Excellent reviews: 4.97★ from 316 reviews** (Judge.me). Huge social proof, currently underused.
- Already offers a **bundle upsell** (washbag + pouch, "save €3/€4")
- Multi-currency, lots of payment methods incl. Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal
- Gift cards available, which are great for Christmas emails

## Gaps and opportunities

### Email (the main pitch)
- **No Klaviyo or dedicated email tool detected.** Only Shopify's basic Forms app. So there are likely **no automated welcome or abandoned-cart flows**. Confirm by signing up and abandoning a cart.
- **No clear sign-up incentive** visible on the homepage (no "10% off your first order").
- The Markets page asks people to "Subscribe to our email!", so **market shoppers are a ready-made list** to turn into online buyers.
- Her 316 reviews could power a **post-purchase review-request flow** and **social-proof emails**.

### Store quick wins (for the audit section of the Loom)
1. **Free shipping threshold is €180**, while most products are €12–€74. Most customers never reach it. Test **€60–€80**.
2. **No real "About / Our Story" page.** The "About" menu only links to a Kashmir Enamels page. Her story (Indian heritage, Dublin markets, handmade) is a big selling point and should have its own page.
3. **Missing SEO basics:** no meta description, and the share description just says "Diya Dublin". Links shared on WhatsApp or Facebook show no description.
4. **Markets page is out of date:** headed "August 2026", with dates listed as "2025" and a "12:00 AM" typo. That looks neglected to shoppers.
5. **Navigation is very long** (30+ categories). Group it into 4–5 top-level menus (Bags, Clothing, Pouches, Home, Gifts).
6. **No Meta (Facebook/Instagram) pixel detected.** If she ever runs Instagram ads, she can't track or retarget visitors. Google tag is present.

## Black Friday / Christmas angle
- An **abandoned-cart flow live before Black Friday (27 Nov)** catches the busiest browsing week of the year.
- **Gift cards + jackets + totes** make a natural "Christmas gift guide" email.
