## The business in one line

> I help small Shopify brands make more money from the traffic they already have.

## What I offer (and nothing else, for now)

1. **Store & Conversion Audit**: the entry offer. About £150–£300, or free for the first 1–2. A short report or screen recording with the top 10 fixes, ranked by impact. Optional paid add-on: I do the easy fixes myself.
2. **Email Revenue Setup**: the main offer. Fixed price, roughly £300–£700 to start.
   - Audit the current email setup and tracking
   - Build 3 core flows: **welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase**
   - Check that tracking and pixels fire correctly
   - Report the revenue the flows brought in after 30 days
3. **Monthly retainer**: email campaigns, flow tweaks, and small store fixes.
4. **Later: ads.** Only once their back end is solid and they trust me.

### Rules I've agreed with myself
- **No Instagram management, branding or content creation.** It dilutes the pitch, takes a lot of time, and the results are hard to prove.
- **Add a service only when 3+ paying clients ask for it.**
- **No custom code.** Theme editor and apps only. Anything needing Liquid or CSS → "that needs a developer."
- **The "not enough" feeling is about inexperience, not offer size.** The fix is reps, not more services.

## Who I pitch (London)

**Good fit:**
- Founder-led, 1–10 people, about 2k–50k Instagram followers
- On Shopify (check with the Wappalyzer extension, or look for "cdn.shopify.com" in the page source)
- Weak email: sign up and add to cart, then check whether a welcome or abandoned-cart email arrives
- Repeat-purchase products: coffee, tea, skincare, candles, pet, snacks, supplements, basics
- Bonus: running ads (check the Meta Ad Library), selling at markets or in shops

**Avoid:** hobby stores with no traffic, big brands with agencies, one-off high-ticket products.

**Where to find them:**
- Markets: Broadway Market, Columbia Road, Spitalfields, Netil Market, Maltby Street, Greenwich, Christmas makers' markets
- The brands stocked in independent shops (Shoreditch, Hackney, Peckham, Islington)
- Instagram: #londonbrand #londonmade #smallbusinessuk #shopsmalllondon
- Eventbrite and Meetup: Shopify, DTC and founder events

## How I reach them

1. **In person at markets** (best channel): buy something, chat, get their email, follow up within 48 hours with a mini-audit and mock-up.
2. **Email to the founder**: short, specific, with screenshots attached.
3. **Instagram DM**: the founder often runs the account.

No LinkedIn videos needed.

**Pre-built mock-ups:** build their welcome or abandoned-cart emails in my own free Klaviyo account (or Canva) with their products and tone, then screenshot them. Max **1–2 hours per prospect**, top 5 only. Reuse one template. Send privately as "a sample of what I'd build for you."

### Weekly rhythm
- Find 10 brands a week → spreadsheet (brand, founder, Shopify Y/N, email gaps, date contacted, follow-ups)
- Full mock-ups for the top 3–5, lighter messages for the rest
- Follow up twice
- One market visit every 1–2 weekends (combine with TikTok filming)
- Legal: keep it one-to-one, say how I found them, stop if asked. Sole traders count as individuals under UK rules.

## Shopify Store Audit Checklist

Score each item ✅ fine / ⚠️ could improve / ❌ missing. Pick the top 10 by impact.

**Email & capture (my main pitch)**
- [ ] Is there an email sign-up pop-up or form, ideally with an offer (e.g. 10% off)?
- [ ] Does a welcome email arrive after signing up? Is it good?
- [ ] Does an abandoned-cart email arrive (add to cart → enter email at checkout → leave)?
- [ ] Is there a post-purchase email beyond the order confirmation?
- [ ] Is the footer sign-up visible?

**First impression (homepage, mobile first)**
- [ ] Is it clear within 5 seconds what they sell and why it's different?
- [ ] Is there one clear main button (e.g. "Shop bestsellers")?
- [ ] Is there a free-shipping threshold or offer bar?
- [ ] Does the page load fast? (Check with Google PageSpeed Insights)

**Product pages**
- [ ] Good photos: multiple angles, in use, lifestyle shots
- [ ] Description covers benefits, not just features, and is easy to skim
- [ ] Reviews are visible (e.g. Judge.me)
- [ ] Price, shipping cost and delivery time are clear
- [ ] Add-to-cart button is visible without scrolling on mobile
- [ ] Upsells or "pairs well with" suggestions

**Trust**
- [ ] Easy-to-find returns policy
- [ ] About page with a founder story or photo
- [ ] Contact details, social proof, press mentions

**Navigation & checkout**
- [ ] Simple menu: can I find the main products in 2 clicks?
- [ ] Working search
- [ ] Checkout has express pay (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, PayPal)
- [ ] No surprise costs at checkout

**Tracking**
- [ ] Meta Pixel installed? (Use the Meta Pixel Helper extension)
- [ ] Google Analytics installed? (Check with Wappalyzer or Tag Assistant)
