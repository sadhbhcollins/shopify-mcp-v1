In Hindi, a diya is a small clay lamp, the kind lit to bring warmth and light into a home. That's exactly what we want every piece we make and stock to do: brighten your day, your table and your everyday bag.

MADE BY HAND.
MADE TO BE LOVED.

Our hand-painted enamels are made by artisans in Srinagar, Kashmir, using lead-free, food-safe paints and traditional designs passed down for generations. No two pieces are ever exactly the same.

Every enamel piece you buy supports over 60 craftspeople, mostly women, earning a fair living wage, and keeps these skills alive for the next generation.
